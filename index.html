MIPSpro software pipelining on r9999

135 TSVC kernels compiled by MIPSpro 7.30 for the R4000, with software pipelining on and off, run on the r9999 out-of-order MIPS core. Overall, SWP is a wash. The kernels where it isn't are explained by two mechanisms in the core, not by the quality of MIPSpro's schedules.

Setup

  • Compiler: MIPSpro 7.30 f77 -n32 -mips3 -r4000 -O3, with and without -SWP:=OFF, run inside IRIX 6.5 under emulation. The source is the Fortran TSVC "vectors" suite (loops.f, mains.f).
  • Harness: the MIPSpro .o files are linked bare-metal with GNU ld through a small n32 shim (harness/): symbol-table fixup, crt0, linker script, and a runtime that times each kernel with CP0 Count. The driver is generated from mains.f with n=100, ld=1000, ntimes=100.
  • Core: the r9999 ooo_core Verilator model with a 32-entry ROB, 512 KB L2 and 37-cycle memory latency (matching the silicon DRAM latency), 1 FP pipe, 1 load/store pipe, and an 8-entry LSU.
  • IPC: instructions per kernel come from the interp_mips ISS, whose Count advances once per instruction. Cycles = 2 × the RTL's Count, which ticks every other cycle. Checksums are identical with and without SWP on all 135 kernels.

About 60% of kernels are within ±3%. The aggregate IPC is pulled down by s451, which spends 16M of the 32M timed cycles in sin/cos at IPC 0.2. The full per-kernel table is results/ipc_table.txt, and the losers and winners with branch-likely and mispredict counts are in results/rank.txt.

Conclusions

Branch-likely. The largest SWP losses all come from the not-taken branch-likely restart (s318). MIPSpro uses b*l .+8 as predication for R4000. To make this cheap, the delay slot of a likely branch could be renamed as a predicated op that selects the old destination register when the branch is not taken, instead of flushing.

LSU. vas (an SWP loss) and s241 (an SWP win) are the same bottleneck viewed from opposite sides: an 8-entry LSU where each store holds its slot until commit, combined with no speculation of loads past unresolved store addresses. Freeing a store's LSU slot when it moves to the store buffer, or adding memory-dependence speculation, should give the no-SWP code most of s241's gain and remove vas's loss. That is also a likely contributor to the low base IPC (geomean 0.8).

Contents

  • kernels/: one page per analyzed kernel.
  • pipetraces/: r9999 gen_html pipetraces. *_focus.html covers two loop trips and is embedded in the kernel pages; the others are 420-instruction windows.
  • harness/: the n32 link shim, runtime, driver generator, MIPSpro objects and their build scripts. make rebuilds tsvc_swp.elf/tsvc_noswp.elf byte-identical to the ones measured.
  • listings/: MIPSpro -S listings with SWP on and off. The #<swps> notes give MIPSpro's own cycles-per-iteration estimates.
  • results/: RTL and ISS per-kernel Counts, branch mispredict logs, the IPC table and the ranking.
  • tools/: ipc.py, rank.py, and findpc/dumprec for reading ooo_core --pipelog dumps.
Core: r9999. Benchmarks: TSVC (Callahan, Dongarra, Levine), Fortran "vectors" version.
